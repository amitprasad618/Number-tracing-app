Privacy Policy

Kids Number Tracing

Last Updated: July 18, 2026

We respect your privacy. Kids Number Tracing is designed to help children learn numbers while keeping their personal information safe.

1. Information We Collect

Kids Number Tracing does not require users to create an account or log in.

We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Location
  • Photos
  • Contacts

2. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for toddlers and preschool children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

3. Permissions

Depending on the features available, the app may request notification permission for medicine reminders or learning reminders. Notifications are generated locally on the device and are not shared with any external server.

4. Internet

The app works offline for its core learning features. Internet access may be required only for downloading future updates or displaying advertisements if enabled in a future version.

5. Advertisements

The current version of the app does not display advertisements. If advertisements are added in a future update using Google AdMob, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Third-Party Services

The app currently does not use third-party analytics or collect personal user data. Future integrations, if any, will be reflected in an updated Privacy Policy.

7. Data Security

All learning progress and application data are stored locally on the user's device. No personal data is transmitted to our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
